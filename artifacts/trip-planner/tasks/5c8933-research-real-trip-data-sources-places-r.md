## Brief
Find APIs/tools that give Trip Fairy real, verifiable trip data instead of LLM guesses: things to do, hotels/places to stay, restaurants, reviews and ratings for each, official links, and booking links (stays, flights, trains, tours). Compare candidates (e.g. Google Places, Foursquare, TripAdvisor Content API, Yelp, OpenStreetMap/Overpass, Wikivoyage, Booking/Expedia/Hotels affiliate APIs, GetYourGuide/Viator, Rome2Rio/Omio) on coverage, cost, rate limits, terms of use (caching, display rules, attribution), and affiliate revenue potential. Recommend which to integrate into city pages, shortlists (BI5), and getting-around, and how they'd replace or validate the current AI-suggested places.
